# Anti-AI Pattern Catalogue (Studio IOS)

The patterns that make copy read as machine-generated, each with a Studio-IOS-context fix. Load this for any de-AI pass or copy review. Adapted from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" and tuned to deck copy.

The method for a de-AI pass:
1. Scan for each pattern below.
2. Rewrite the flagged span, keeping the real creative content and any true numbers.
3. Final pass — ask *"what still reads as AI here?"*, name it, fix it.

---

### 1. Undue emphasis on significance / legacy

AI puffs importance by claiming a thing "represents", "marks a pivotal moment", or "reflects a broader shift".

> ❌ This badge marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of the brand's visual identity, reflecting a broader shift toward heritage-led design.

> ✅ The badge replaces the wordmark as the collection's lead mark. It's built for embroidery, so it carries onto product without redraw.

---

### 2. Superficial "-ing" analyses

Present-participle clauses tacked on to fake depth.

> ❌ The palette runs charcoal to bone, symbolising restraint and reflecting the brand's combat-sports roots while reinforcing its premium positioning.

> ✅ The palette is charcoal and bone — two colours, so every piece in the range reads as one family on the shelf.

Delete the "-ing" clause. If the point it gestured at matters, state it as a fact with a specific.

---

### 3. Promotional / advertisement language

Neutral-tone failure: adjectives doing the argument's job.

> ❌ A stunning, dynamic badge that elevates the collection with striking visual impact.

> ✅ The badge holds at 15mm and drops to a single colour for the woven version. It carries the collection without competing with the garment.

Every hype adjective is a missing specific. Find the specific.

---

### 4. Copula avoidance

AI dodges plain "is/are" with "serves as", "stands as", "acts as", "represents".

> ❌ The accent mark serves as a unifying device across the range.

> ✅ The accent mark is the through-line — it appears on every piece at the same 8mm scale.

---

### 5. Negative parallelism / tailing negations

> ❌ It's not just a logo — it's a statement of intent.
> ❌ The options come from the brief, no guesswork.

> ✅ The logo is the brief's answer: one mark the audience recognises instantly.
> ✅ Every option here traces directly to a line in the brief.

Cut "not just X but Y". Say what the thing is.

---

### 6. Rule-of-three padding

Empty triads that sound comprehensive and say nothing.

> ❌ The collection delivers innovation, inspiration, and impact.

> ✅ The collection runs on three graphic anchors instead of nine disconnected marks.

Keep a triad only when all three items are real and load-bearing ("graphic language, trend framework and seasonal direction"). Otherwise cut to the one that matters.

---

### 7. AI vocabulary

delve, leverage, showcase, underscore, boasts, foster, testament, tapestry, vibrant, pivotal, crucial, robust, seamless, elevate, in the heart of, nestled, landscape (abstract), journey (figurative). Full list in `lexicon.md`.

> ❌ This collection showcases a vibrant tapestry of graphic elements that underscore the brand's robust heritage.

> ✅ The collection is built from four marks that all trace back to the 1998 academy crest.

---

### 8. Vague / weasel attribution

> ❌ Studies show that graphic-led product outsells plain product.

> ✅ On the FUJI work, sell-through on the graphics-led pieces ran ~2x the plain-shell pieces.

Studio IOS cites its own work or says nothing. No anonymous "experts" or "research".

---

### 9. Em-dash overuse (the nuanced one)

Studio IOS *uses* the spaced dash — it's house style. The AI tell is **frequency**, not the dash itself: every sentence hinging on one, or two in a single sentence.

> ❌ The badge — built for embroidery — anchors the range — and it scales to a single colour — which keeps production simple.

> ✅ The badge anchors the range and is built for embroidery. It scales to a single colour, which keeps production simple.

Rule of thumb: ≤ 1 dash per paragraph, and only where it attaches a genuine specific.

---

### 10. Symmetrical rhythm / sing-song

Three sentences of the same length and shape in a row reads as generated.

> ❌ The badge anchors the range. The palette unifies the pieces. The mark ties it together.

> ✅ The badge anchors the range. Around it, a two-colour palette keeps every piece reading as one family, and a single accent mark ties the odd pieces back in.

Break the pattern: one short, one long.

---

### 11. False ranges

> ❌ Everything from the badge to the buying cycle has been considered.

> ✅ Two things were locked first: the badge system and the colourway. Everything else resolves off those.

---

### 12. Rhetorical-question and throat-clearing openers

> ❌ What makes a collection unforgettable? For [brand], it starts with identity.
> ❌ In today's crowded market, standing out is harder than ever.

> ✅ The collection is built around one recognisable mark, applied the same way on every piece.

Open on the decision. Delete the wind-up.
