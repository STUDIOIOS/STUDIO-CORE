## 2. The trigger: the Kimura round

**March 2026 Series A into Adapt from Kimura Group Limited**, a special-purpose vehicle created to facilitate investment on behalf of fitness and lifestyle entrepreneurs **James Smith**, **Diren Kartal** and **Paul Olima**, alongside other investors. Legal counsel: Lester Aldridge (Partner Jamie Earley, Solicitor Janet Stocker). Stated purpose: expanding presence in the UK and internationally.

Two cautions before this goes in an email:

- **Verify the spelling of Diren Kartal.** The law firm's announcement renders it "Kartel". Getting an investor's name wrong in the opening line is fatal.
- **Do not quote a round size.** Third-party aggregators show "$51.1K raised", which is almost certainly a Companies House share-allotment artefact rather than the round. No credible figure is public. Reference the round, never the number.

### Why the investor list is the whole strategy

James Smith, Diren Kartal and Paul Olima are audience owners. Fitness and lifestyle entrepreneurs with large, highly engaged online communities. They are not apparel operators, not supply-chain people, not brand builders.

So Adapt has just bought **reach**, immediately, without earning it drop by drop. What they have not bought is craft capacity or brand infrastructure.

That is the condition under which premium minimal brands break. Reach creates volume pressure. Volume pressure asks for more SKUs, more collaborations, a faster drop calendar, new territories. Every one of those is an invitation to get louder, because loud is easy to brief and restraint is not. The brands Adapt differentiates against are loud partly because loud scales without a system.

**Our offer is not more design. It is holding the line while they scale.** Brand guide and design kits are the instrument that lets a small team and a fast-growing distribution network ship consistent, restrained, premium product without one person art-directing every item.

## 3. The Grant problem — the tactical crux

Grant currently owns product design, wholesale custom artwork, product photography and overall visual direction. One person, all of it.

Post-raise, with international expansion and wholesale growth, two things are true at once:

1. He is the bottleneck. He cannot art-direct expansion, wholesale custom work, photography and hold brand consistency simultaneously.
2. He is the person most likely to kill this. Any approach that reads as "we design better than your founder" gets forwarded to Grant and dies there.

**Every line of the email must survive being forwarded to Grant.** That is the test to apply.

The framing that passes it: a design kit and brand guide are the system that scales Grant's taste. Multiplication, not replacement. Adam gains commercial capacity; Grant keeps authorship. The line doing this work in the draft is *"lets Grant's eye scale past Grant's hours."*

Send to Adam. He is CEO, majority holder, and owns strategy and partnerships, so he is the buyer. But write for two readers.

## 4. Register decision — premium heritage, not combat sports

Adapt is a BJJ brand, so the reflex is our combat-sports register. **That reflex is wrong here and would actively repel them.**

The combat register is "grit, grain and gothic rage": distorted Spartan skulls, warped Latin text, anarchic brush marks, all-caps fight-poster closers. That is precisely the aesthetic Adapt built their brand against.

**Recommendation: premium heritage register for all Adapt-facing work,** including the 2027 board. Elevated, composed, heritage-into-fashion, restraint over decoration. Adapt is the first combat-sports account that needs the heritage voice.

The framing pair to resolve is not grit ↔ refinement. It is **mat culture ↔ elevated lifestyle**. Same move as ESPN (broadcast → lifestyle) and WEC (technical precision → elevated apparel), pointed at jiu-jitsu.

Per `studio-ios-voice`, this is a judgement call the skill says to bring to Tom rather than decide silently. Flagging it as a decision, with a recommendation.

## 5. Positioning: reduction as commercial discipline

The single idea the outreach turns on:

> **Our value is not adding graphics. It is editing them. Restraint is the expensive discipline.**

This reframes our entire portfolio for an anti-graphic brand without misrepresenting a thing. It also pre-empts Adapt's likely objection to a combat-sports studio, which is "you make the loud stuff we avoid."

Supporting proof, re-cut for restraint:

- **Three graphic anchors instead of nine disconnected ones** (Fuji season we directed). This already exists in `sequences.json` T2 step 3 as a throwaway. For Adapt it is the headline. It is a restraint argument wearing the wrong clothes.
- **17% YoY uplift** on that season. Editing produced the number, not addition.
- **Assets still in range a year later.** Design investment that did not depreciate after one drop. Currently in T3 step 2. Speaks directly to a brand that wants to look considered rather than seasonal.

**Why Sekai is the right process piece.** It is the closest thing in our portfolio to Adapt's own aesthetic target: curated restraint, Japanese heritage reference, minimal execution. Showing *process* rather than outcome is what disarms a founder-designer.

**Why We The People carries the numbers.** Storytelling depth without illustration density, and it is the one that answers Adam's commercial question. Attach the figures.

## 8. The 2027 ADAPT board — highest leverage, real risk

A speculative board named after their brand, in their aesthetic, unrequested, is close to unignorable. It is the strongest single move available.

**Two failure modes to manage:**

1. **Presumption.** "We redesigned you without being asked" insults a founder-designer. See section 3.
2. **Devaluation.** Free pitching sets the price of our thinking at zero.

**Mitigation — frame it as a trend direction applied, not a proposed collection.** It is our point of view on where elevated combat-sport apparel goes in 2027, built with Adapt as the reference case. That framing keeps authorship with Grant, positions us as trend intelligence rather than replacement designers, and is honest about what it is.

**Naming.** "ADAPT" as a board title is flat. Premium-heritage convention is the badge-concept format: an ALL CAPS title anchored in one real, checkable fact. Per house rules those anchors must be genuine, so they need supplying rather than inventing. Directions worth exploring once we have real anchors: the discipline of the plain white gi; the belt as a record of time served; Japanese textile restraint; the uniform as the opposite of a costume.
