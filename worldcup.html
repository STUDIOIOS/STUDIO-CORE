⚔️ Face-off duels — how it works

You start with 1,000 tokens. When one of your teams plays a rival manager's team, bet against them on the result.
  1. Sign in — pick your name and set a 4-digit PIN (so only you can place your bets).
  2. Challenge a rival on any match where your teams meet, and stake some tokens on your team.
  3. They accept or decline. Bets lock at kick-off and settle automatically at full time.
  4. Win → you take their stake · draw → stakes returned · lose → they take yours.
Your final token balance = your slice of the £100 prize pot. Tap Standings any time to see the leaderboard and projected winnings.
